We’re halfway between Meat & the Next Act on 104 Street and The Strathcona Hotel on Gateway Blvd, but you won’t find us on the front street. The only access to our shop is in the back alley, tucked in behind the Bondi Beach.

The alley is occasionally hard to access due to delivery trucks, poorly placed road construction signs, and annual, but there is quite often a free parking spot right in front of the door. The Old Strathcona Farmers' Market parking lot is less than 200m away. Plenty of street parking is available throughout the neighborhood.
